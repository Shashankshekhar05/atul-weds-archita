# Royal Wedding Invitation — Dr. Atul Kumar & Archita Malik
॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥

An elegant, interactive Indian wedding invitation designed as a single, self-contained web page (`index.html`) crafted to feel like luxury printed stationery brought to life.

---

## 🌟 Features & Highlights

1. **Self-Contained Single File**:
   - Everything is contained within [index.html](file:///c:/Users/shashank%20shekhar/Downloads/Wedding/index.html) (HTML5, CSS in `<style>`, JS in `<script>`, inline SVG illustrations, zero external JavaScript frameworks).
   - Embedded Base64 audio + local MP3 support.

2. **Royal Stationery Aesthetic**:
   - **Palette**: Deep royal maroon (`#4A0E17`), antique gold foil (`#D4AF37`), and warm ivory parchment (`#FAF7F2`).
   - **Stationery Details**: Subtle embossed gold dot texture, arched Mughal card architecture, fine gold filigree double borders, and Ganesha gold vector seal.
   - **Dark Mode**: Automatically respects device preference and includes an interactive toggle switching to a deep royal wine & champagne gold palette.

3. **Interactions & Moments**:
   - **Interactive Maroon Envelope**:
     - 3D perspective fold flap with a custom wax seal stamp bearing the **"AA"** monogram.
     - Tapping the seal opens the flap in 3D, slides out the invitation letter, and triggers a cascade of realistic falling marigold and rose petals.
   - **Jitter-Free 3D Card Tilt with Specular Sheen**:
     - Smoothly tilts in 3D following mouse movement on desktop or mobile gyroscope on mobile devices.
   - **Gold Foil Scratch Card**:
     - Conceals the wedding date and timings under a sparkling brushed gold foil canvas.
     - Guests scratch it off using a finger on touchscreens or a mouse on desktop.
     - Reaching ~55-60% scratched automatically reveals the full date with a celebratory burst of rose and marigold petals.
   - **Minimalist Luxury Live Countdown**:
     - Counts down live to **Monday, 14 December 2026, 10:00 AM IST** (`2026-12-14T10:00:00+05:30`).
   - **3D Flip Cards for Ceremonies**:
     - Chronological order:
       1. **Haldi Ceremony** (Friday, 11 December 2026, 11:00 AM onwards) — Radiant Sun Motif
       2. **Mehendi & Sangeet** (Sunday, 13 December 2026, 4:00 PM onwards) — Henna Paisley Motif
       3. **The Royal Wedding** (Monday, 14 December 2026, Baraat 10:00 AM, Preetibhoj 11:00 AM, Vidaai 6:00 PM) — Sacred Lotus Motif
     - Each card flips in 3D on tap/click or keyboard space/enter to view exact timings, venue, dress code color swatches, Google Maps directions, and Google Calendar links in IST.
   - **Our Story (2026 – Forever Begins)**:
     - Auspicious single commemorative milestone:
       - **2026 – Forever Begins** with sacred Varmala garland artwork: *"Surrounded by elders' blessings at M. S. Farm, two hearts unite in the sacred bond of marriage."*
